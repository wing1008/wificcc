## Direction A — 한 줄의 재배치 (one line, re-arranged)
- **Idea:** the visitor's one typed Korean sentence is the protagonist. Scrolling takes its letters apart and re-lays them as the material of what gets made: tiles of a game level, cells of a program window, bars of a tool. The last scene gives the line back to the visitor as the ask.
- **Layout system:** 12-col on 1920 with 80 px margins; huge type held on the left 7 cols, the "made thing" builds in the right 5; one input rule (2 px ink line) carried through every scene as the horizon.
- **Type pairing:** Noto Serif KR 900 display (glyphs become physical tiles, so they need weight) + IBM Plex Sans KR body + IBM Plex Mono labels (INPUT / OUTPUT numbering).
- **Motion concept:** glyph → tile reflow along timed arcs (scroll-scrubbed, glide); the deleted words of the sentence fly off one by one. Rest state: caret blinks, paper grain, a slow raking light across the sheet.
- **Colour grade:** warm paper #F1ECE2, ink #14110D, one signal orange #FF4A1C; paper grain multiply; light from top-left with soft cast shadows under landed tiles (to be added in build).
- **Signature transition:** the "reflow" — a whole composed thing (a game level) collapses back into letters which re-flow into the next thing (a program window), the input rule staying fixed as the hinge.
- **Keeps / breaks:** keeps T1 (the prompt line is the hero — visitor's reason: "I could start now") and T4 (range shown by the same letters becoming different things). Breaks T2 (no dark glow; bright paper — to be told apart, T8) and T3 (no screenshots; the output is drawn from the visitor's own words — proof by act, not by gallery). T5/T6 left out: INTAKE has no proof or prices; not invented.
- **Section reference (level to reach):** section/illoca — one world, sections that slide over each other as sheets and hand over with tilt and light.

## Direction B — 불 켜지는 작업대 (the workbench that lights up)
- **Idea:** a dark room; each section is one made thing (program, game, tool) that exists first as thin warm wireframe and turns solid where a beam of light passes.
- **Layout:** full-bleed stage, object centre-right, thin display type bottom-left, caption right, 5-part progress rule along the bottom.
- **Type:** IBM Plex Sans KR 200 display + IBM Plex Mono.
- **Motion:** scroll drives the beam across the object (wire → solid), slow camera dolly between objects; alive at rest via dust and grain.
- **Colour grade:** near-black #07080A, warm amber light #FFC98A / #FFE7C4, grain.
- **Signature transition:** the beam leaves the object, the room goes dark, the next object's wireframe is already there when the beam comes back.
- **Keeps / breaks:** keeps T2 in spirit (dark = tech) but warm, not purple; keeps T3 as built objects rather than screenshots; drops T1 (no prompt as hero). Risk: still the trade's dark default (T8).
- **Section reference:** section/mclaren-w1 — one object per section revealed out of darkness, slow dark→light rhythm.

## Direction C — 만든 것들의 지도 (map of things made)
- **Idea:** the site is one infinite pale canvas of hundreds of small made things; scroll zooms into one tile until it fills the screen as a section, then pulls out along a drawn line to the next.
- **Layout:** free canvas, dot grid, one enlarged tile at a time; heavy headline bottom-left.
- **Type:** Gothic A1 900 + IBM Plex Mono.
- **Motion:** camera zoom-through-tile; line path drawn between tiles.
- **Colour grade:** pale blue-grey #E9EEF6, navy ink, cobalt #2440FF.
- **Signature transition:** zoom into a tile → tile becomes the viewport → pull back out along a cobalt line.
- **Keeps / breaks:** keeps T3/T4 (gallery and range, literally) and T5 by quantity; breaks T2 (bright). Risk: the zoomed tile is a stock SaaS card, close to floor.
- **Section reference:** section/radian — scattered images linked by a drawn line, calm frames alternating with full takeovers.

## Pick: A
- **Who comes [DRAFT]:** individuals and companies with an idea for a program, a game or a tool — any field — who don't know how (or don't have the team) to build it. Korean-speaking, on desktop.
- **What they should do [DRAFT]:** write their idea as one line and send it.
- **Why A serves them:** A's main idea *is* that action — the site demonstrates on the visitor's own kind of sentence that one line is enough, and the last scene hands the input line back to them. It answers "does my odd idea fit?" (no fixed field — the same letters become a game, a program, a tool) without invented logos, numbers or screenshots, and it needs no stand-in photos. Bright paper + Korean display type is obviously unlike the dark-glow trade default and unlike the floor templates.
- **What must rise in build:** light (raking light and cast shadows on landed glyph-tiles, WebGL displacement/grain on paper), timed choreography of the reflow, and the scene-to-scene hand-over — these are where A falls short of illoca.
