# PLAN — picked direction A (한 줄의 재배치)

## Main idea (apart from its expression)
One line of words is all it takes: the visitor's own kind of sentence comes apart and its letters become the material of whatever is made — a game, a program, a tool — and at the end the line is handed back to the visitor to write their own.

(Layout, type, colour, motion, transitions and copy may all be worked out again later.)

## Facts every later build keeps the same
- **Business name:** 온갖 [DRAFT] — INTAKE.md has no name; working wordmark only, confirm before publishing.
- **What is offered:** an AI startup that makes programs, games and other software with AI, across all fields; no specific field. (INTAKE.md, BRIEF.md)
- **Site language:** Korean only. Desktop browsers only.
- **Who comes [DRAFT]:** people and companies with an idea for a program, game or tool, in any field, who lack the means or team to build it.
- **What they should do [DRAFT]:** send their idea as one line (the single ask of the page).
- **Prices:** left out (not in INTAKE.md; the brief asks no action that needs them).
- **Contact:** left out (not in INTAKE.md). The ask is shown as an idea-line form; where it is delivered is to be confirmed with the customer.
- **Proof / trust:** left out — INTAKE.md gives none; no invented logos, numbers or testimonials.

## Image route: no images
Type, drawing and motion made in code (glyph tiles, code-built forms, WebGL light/grain over the paper). Why: the business makes software, so there is no real place, people or product to photograph; licensed stock photos would be stand-ins that could pass as the company's own team or office, and Codex is not installed in this environment (`which codex` → none). The main idea is literally letters becoming things, so code-made material is the honest and the strongest route. REPLACE.md (build phase) will therefore list no stand-in images, only the [DRAFT] name and the sample sentences the customer may want to swap for real projects.
